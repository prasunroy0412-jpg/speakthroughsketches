About Me

Where engineering precision meets visual storytelling.

Art is how I speak

I’m Prasun — an engineer, researcher and artist from Assam, India.

My work moves between precision and expression. Engineering has taught me to observe structure, proportion and detail; art gives me a way to turn those observations into stories.

I work across digital art, graphite, watercolour and other visual experiments. I am especially drawn to portraits, architecture, landscapes and places that carry a memory. Sometimes an artwork begins with a photograph from a journey; sometimes it starts with a face, a line or simply an idea I want to explore.

Speak Through Sketches is my personal space for bringing those different parts together. The gallery holds finished and experimental work, while the timelapses show how a blank canvas gradually becomes an image. In Stories, I share more of the thinking, references and process behind selected pieces.

For me, the process matters as much as the finished artwork. A sketch can preserve the atmosphere of a place, a watercolour can turn a photograph into something more personal, and a digital painting can simplify a scene until only the parts that matter remain.

This is Speak Through Sketches — a growing record of the things I notice, make and remember.

Prasun Roy
Engineer by day. Artist by night.
